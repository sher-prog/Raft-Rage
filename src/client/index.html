THE RIVER RULES

A little teamwork.
A lot of splash.

  1. Stand to steer. Walk around the rim. Fire at the back to go forward; fire on the left to slide right.
  2. Meet your Hippo. A big SPOUT kicks your whole raft away from where you stand.
  3. Whack and block. When rivals get close, whack to stun. Whack during their windup to block.

Keyboard: A / D to walk · W to spout · S to whack.
On phones, use the big touch buttons.

This first checkpoint connects friends in a lobby. Racing is the next build.
