THE RIVER RULES

A little teamwork.
A lot of splash.

  1. Stand to steer. Walk around the rim. Fire at the back to go forward; fire on the left to slide right.
  2. Pick your animal. Hippo spouts, Frog charges and releases, Bear pushes, and Capybara whirls. Each kicks the raft away from where it stands.
  3. Whack and block. When rivals get close, whack to stun. Whack during their windup to block.

Keyboard: A / D to walk · W to spout · S to whack.
On phones, use the big touch buttons.

Stand at the stern to go forward. Collect crates for Speed, a Bubble shield, or a throwable Log. Draft behind the leader for SLIPSTREAM. Keep an eye on heat, charge and stamina.
